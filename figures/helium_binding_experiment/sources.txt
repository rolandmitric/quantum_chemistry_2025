Die Bindungsenergie des Heliumatoms

Scientific sources

J. Franck and P. Knipping, "Die Ionisierungsspannungen des Heliums",
Physikalische Zeitschrift 20, 481–488 (1919).
The apparatus is described in the paper; the double-ionization measurement
is discussed on pp. 486–488. The reported mean threshold is 79.5 ± 0.3 V.
https://archive.org/details/physikalische-zeitschrift-vol-20/page/n585/mode/2up

NIST, Atomic Data for Helium.
First ionization energy: 24.587387 eV.
Second ionization energy: 54.417760 eV.
Their sum is 79.005147 eV, rounded on the slide to 79.005 eV.
The ground-state energy relative to a bare helium nucleus and two free
electrons has the opposite sign, approximately −2.9034 Hartree.
https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/heliumtable1.htm

The interpretation follows pp. 487–488: clean helium shows a weak additional
kink near the reported direct double-ionization threshold of 79.5 V, on an
already substantial ionization current. The paper separately notes that
contamination can produce a downward kink; the slide illustrates the clean
gas case. "Direct double ionization" denotes removal of both bound electrons
from neutral helium in one collision, not sequential ionization through He+.
